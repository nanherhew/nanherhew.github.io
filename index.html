COMMISSION TERMS.

01 / PAYMENT

Payment is required before the artwork is started. Prices listed on this website are starting prices and may change depending on the complexity of the request.

02 / PROCESS

After receiving your request, I will review the details and contact you to confirm the commission, price and estimated timeframe.

03 / REVISIONS

Minor revisions are allowed during the sketch stage. Major changes requested after approval may require an additional fee.

04 / DEADLINES

Commission completion times may vary depending on my current queue, the complexity of the artwork and other circumstances. I will communicate significant delays.

05 / REFUNDS

Refund requests are handled individually depending on the current stage of the commission.

06 / USAGE

The finished artwork remains the property of the artist. The commissioner receives personal-use rights unless commercial usage is explicitly agreed upon.

07 / NSFW

Mature commissions may be accepted depending on the subject and content. Requests outside my boundaries will be declined.

08 / ARTIST RIGHTS

I reserve the right to decline a commission request. I may display completed artwork in my portfolio unless a private commission arrangement has been agreed upon.
